Help with CartScope

Capture a product after opening its page and clicking CartScope's toolbar icon. Choose Capture this page, review the details, and save. If metadata is unavailable, add the item manually.

Installation

Extract the runtime ZIP. Open chrome://extensions, enable Developer mode, choose Load unpacked, and select the folder containing manifest.json. Pin CartScope. The browser demo cannot read another tab.

Report a problem or give feedback

Ratings and written feedback

Issue tracker · Sprint 2.E Fielding & Support

Include your extension version, Chrome version, operating system, steps, expected result and actual result. For usability feedback, include task outcomes, time taken and 1–5 ratings, plus confusing steps and requested improvements. Public issues and Store reviews are public: omit private order details, payment information and unredacted backups. Reference testers can also reply privately to the team's invitation email.

Data controls

Use Settings to export/import a backup or delete workspace data. Sample and real workspaces are separate. Removing the extension does not delete exported files. See the privacy policy.

Current limits

Offers are captured or entered by you. Shipping and tax must be confirmed. Watches evaluate on an offer update, order status is manual, and the planner uses entered financing terms. Live catalog discovery, unattended polling, carrier updates, screenshot recognition and occasion folders remain planned.
