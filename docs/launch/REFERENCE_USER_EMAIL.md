Hi [first name],

We're inviting ten reference users to try CartScope Launch Candidate 0.1.2, our Chrome shopping side panel. Your reference ID is **[R01–R10]**. It keeps products from different stores together so you can check the same item, compare the costs you know, and keep a budget and useful notes.

[Release downloads and release notes](https://github.com/qarteu/cartscope/releases) · [Source and instructions](https://github.com/qarteu/cartscope) · [Try the browser demo](https://qarteu.github.io/cartscope/)

To install the downloaded preview locally:

1. Select **CartScope Launch Candidate 0.1.2** in the release index, download `cartscope-launch-0.1.2.zip`, and unzip it into a folder you'll keep. Confirm the **PREVIEW 0.1.2** badge in CartScope.
2. Open `chrome://extensions` in Chrome 116 or newer. Enable Developer mode and choose Load unpacked.
3. Select the unzipped folder containing `manifest.json`, then pin CartScope from Chrome's Extensions menu.
4. Open one product page, click the CartScope toolbar icon, and choose Capture this page. Check the product and variant before saving. You can start with the labeled sample workspace instead.

Please spend about 15–20 minutes trying the [task sheet](https://github.com/qarteu/cartscope/blob/main/docs/FEEDBACK_PLAN.md). No purchase is needed. Try installation/capture, a second store, quantity and budget changes, a checked comparison, a watch, a fictitious manual order, the payment planner, and a backup. Mark anything you did not attempt. The browser demo cannot capture shopping tabs or deliver desktop notifications, so identify which version/mode you used.

For each task, tell us whether it was completed, completed with help, blocked, or not attempted. Record elapsed seconds if you can; mark estimates. Then rate these from 1 to 5:

- Ease of use: very difficult → very easy.
- Information clarity: very unclear → very clear.
- Usefulness: not useful → very useful.
- Confidence in the displayed information: very low → very high.

Please also tell us what confused you, what information was missing, and the single change you would want next. Critical feedback and failed tasks are useful. Submit the [structured feedback form](https://github.com/qarteu/cartscope/issues/new?template=alpha-feedback.yml), or reply privately to this email using the same task/rating fields. Include your reference ID, actual version, Chrome/OS version, and whether your data was real, manual, or demo. GitHub issues are public: leave out personal order numbers, addresses, payment details, and unredacted backups.

CartScope records information you capture or enter. It does not automatically search stores, monitor prices while you are away, update shipments, or check out. Watches need another capture or offer update. Sample prices and orders are examples. Your workspace stays in local browser storage; external images and websites you open still contact their hosts. Read our [privacy policy](https://qarteu.github.io/cartscope/privacy.html) and [support instructions](https://qarteu.github.io/cartscope/support.html).

Please reply by **[date and time, Pacific]**. Participation and feedback are optional.
